## What's new in V1.1

V1.1 builds on V1.0 with three capabilities:

### 1. Meaning Clarification
- **That's Not What I Meant** on the Results screen
- Clarify intent in English or rewrite in French
- Re-analysis returns updated Results without going back to Landing

### 2. Context-Aware Suggestions
- **Suggested Messages** replaces single "Ready to Send"
- **Informal French** and **Formal French** with independent copy buttons
- 3-column comparison table: Your Sentence | Informal | Formal
- Separate informal and formal explanations in **Why These Changes?**

### 3. My French Toolbox
- After correction, a **separate AI task** performs full linguistic analysis of the original + corrected sentences
- Extracts **all meaningful vocabulary** (unchanged, corrected, multi-word expressions)
- Stored in **lemma (dictionary) form** with **surface forms** preserved (e.g. lemma `être`, surface `suis`)
- Multi-word expressions kept together (`parce que`, `est-ce que`, `il y a`)
- Browser `localStorage` — dashboard on Landing, category vocabulary browser
- Deduped by lemma + meaning + part of speech; merges surfaces and examples over time

## AI architecture (V1.1)

Two separate prompts run sequentially:

| Task | Prompt | Returns |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1. Sentence Correction | Correction | understood, informal/formal suggestions, changes, explanations, ratings |
| 2. Vocabulary Extraction | Linguistic analysis | lemma, surface, meaning, partOfSpeech, example for every meaningful item |

Correction completes first; vocabulary extraction analyzes **both** the learner's sentence and the corrected versions.
